# Privacy

AMUG has no account, analytics, advertising, or Internet permission. Core mug
control is local Bluetooth Low Energy.

AMUG stores app-private data on the device: remembered mug names/BLE addresses,
preferences, presets, recent telemetry/session history, and learned temperature
choices. Android backup/device transfer is disabled. Forgetting a mug deletes
its related local records; Settings can clear history and learned suggestions.

Diagnostics are shared only after the user explicitly chooses Share. The report
omits the BLE address. AMUG does not copy diagnostics to the clipboard
automatically.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/logix727/amug/issues
